Questions, answered.

Something missing? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is Toomu really free?+

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no paid tier and no account to create.

How do I install the APK?+

Download it on your phone and open the file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser; say yes, then tap Install. From then on Toomu installs its own updates.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?+

Google doesn't allow apps that download from YouTube. Every release is published on GitHub instead.

Which sites does it work with?+

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook links download straight away. For other sites, open the page in Toomu's browser and it picks up the videos and audio that play there. Protected (DRM) streams such as Netflix can't be saved.

Can I download in 4K or as MP3?+

Yes, whenever the video is available in 4K. Pick "Audio only" to save just the sound for the Music tab.

Does the Windows app need anything else?+

No. On first launch Toomu fetches the tools it needs to download and convert video, then keeps them up to date by itself.
